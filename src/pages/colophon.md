This site was designed and built by [Steel Wagstaff](http://steelwagstaff.com) to satisfy the degree requirements for his Ph.D. in English (literary studies) from the University of Madison-Wisconsin.

This site is powered by the [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) content management system to publish this site and a custom theme, called 'Objectivist,' built expressly for this project. The site incorporates three [Adobe Typekit](https://typekit.com) typefaces: [Brandon Grotesque](https://typekit.com/fonts/brandon-grotesque) is used for headings, [Calluna](https://typekit.com/fonts/calluna) is used for the body text, and [Prenton](https://typekit.com/fonts/prenton) is used for block quotes. Except where noted, all of the visual resources on this site were made using high-resolution scanning equipment.

Grateful acknowledgments to the estates, families, and literary executors of the poets featured on this site who have been both gracious in extending permissions and encouraging this project in various ways over the past several years: Marilyn Kane and Dan and Barbara Nordby \[for Carl and Leah Rakosi\]; Linda Oppen \[for George and Mary Oppen\]; Bob and Susan Arnold, Kori Oberle and Marilee Lee at the Hoard Historical Museum, and Amy Lutzke at the Dwight Foster Public Library \[for Lorine Niedecker\]; Aaron Kerner of Black Sparrow Books \[for Charles Reznikoff\]; Daphne Williams Fox \[for William Carlos Williams\]; John Halliday \[for Basil Bunting\]; and Musical Observations, Inc. \[for Louis Zukofsky\].
