The best available online resources for scholars and general readers interested in Zukofsky's writing is [Z-Site](http://www.z-site.net/), an extensive "Companion to the Works of Louis Zukofsky" maintained from Malta by [Jeffrey Twichell-Waas](mailto:jtwaas@gmail.com). Twichell-Waas' [editor's notes](http://www.z-site.net/editors-notes/) provide a richly detailed and very useful description of that site's contents and objectives. Rather than reproducing the many hours of painstaking labor that have gone into producing and maintaining that resource here, I happily refer the reader there.
