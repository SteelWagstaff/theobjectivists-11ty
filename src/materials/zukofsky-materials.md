## Major Collections

### Louis Zukofsky Papers at the Harry Ransom Humanities Research Center at UT-Austin

The primary collection of Zukofsky's manuscripts and papers are housed at the Harry Ransom Humanities Research Center at the University of Texas, Austin.

#### Finding Aid

A detailed finding aid for the HRHRC's Zukofsky holdings can be viewed [online](http://www.lib.utexas.edu/taro/uthrc/00138/hrc-00138.html). In addition, Marcella Booth published [a detailed catalogue of the Louis Zukofsky Manuscript Collection](http://www.worldcat.org/oclc/251487437) in 1975, which was followed by [a lengthy supplement](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=inu.30000003241985;view=1up;seq=113) published in UT-Austin's _Library Chronicle_ by Cathy Henderson in 1987 (pdf downloaded from the HathiTrust available [here](http://theobjectivists.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2014/11/cathy_henderson_supplement_zukofsky_catalogue.pdf)).

### Other Collections

Z-site has published a [very detailed description of collections](http://www.z-site.net/biblio-research/lz-manuscripts-papers/) which hold Zukofsky's manuscripts and papers. The Zukofsky scholar Barry Ahearn has also prepared a finding aid for Zukofsky correspondence, which is available [online](http://www2.tulane.edu/liberal-arts/english/ahearn/zukofsky_search_form.cfm).

## Literary Executor

Louis and Celia Zukofsky had one son, the violinist Paul Zukofsky, who [died in June 2017](https://jacket2.org/commentary/paul-zukofsky-1943%E2%80%932017). Following Paul's death, the board of Musical Observations, Inc. became the executor of Louis, Celia, and Paul's estates, and now handles all [permission requests](http://www.musicalobservations.com/permissions/index.html) to reproduce any of Louis or Celia Zukofsky's published or unpublished material.
