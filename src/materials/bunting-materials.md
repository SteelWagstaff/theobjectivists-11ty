## Major Collections

The major collections of Basil Bunting materials are located at Durham University and SUNY-Buffalo, with smaller Bunting-related collections at Yale University (in New Haven, Connecticut), the Harry Ransom Center (at UT-Austin), The Archive for New Poetry (at University of California, San Diego),  Washington University (in St. Louis), Miami University ...

### Basil Bunting Poetry Archive at Durham University

The largest collection of Bunting-related material is held in the Basil Bunting Poetry Archive owned by Durham University Special Collections in North East England.

#### Finding Aid

Durham University maintains [a detailed finding aid](http://reed.dur.ac.uk/xtf/view?docId=ark/32150_s1ww72bb48x.xml#qxj-415) for the material in the Basil Bunting Poetry Archive.

### Bunting Letters at SUNY-Buffalo

#### The Bunting Pickard Collection

> The Bunting Pickard Collection contains approximately 1,000 volumes from Basil Bunting’s personal library, many signed, inscribed, and/or with notes; the notebook manuscript of Briggflatts; 64 letters from Bunting to Tom Pickard; manuscripts and typescripts of works by Pickard; correspondence; materials relating to the Morden Tower reading series; cassette recordings of Bunting; and miscellaneous photographs.<sup id="fnref-1"><a href="#fn-1">[1]</a></sup>

#### Finding Aid:

http://purl.org/net/findingaids/view?docId=ead/poetry/ubpo\_pcms0018.xml

#### Kenneth Cox Collection

The Kenneth Cox collections contains several letters exchanged between 1966 and 1984 by Bunting and Kenneth Cox, an English literary critic.

#### Finding Aid:

http://libweb1.lib.buffalo.edu:8080/findingaids/view?docId=ead//poetry/ubpo\_pcms0054.xml

### Bunting Letters at the Harry Ransom Center

Several letters from Bunting to Zukofsky are included in the Louis Zukofsky Collection held at the Harry Ransom Center at the University of Texas-Austin

#### Finding Aid:

http://norman.hrc.utexas.edu/fasearch/findingAid.cfm?eadid=00138&kw=zukofsky

### Bunting Letters at the Beinecke
